The owner-facing overview is the [README](../README.md); this is the detail per
package: where it comes from in KaiT2en (or that it's this repo's own), and what
was tested, on which Mac, when. "Works" means tested on a MacBookPro16,1 unless a
row says otherwise.

### Notes from testing (MacBookPro16,1)

- The T2 SMC keeps manual fan mode across reboots. To hand fans back: stop the
  daemon, then write `0` to each `fanN_target` of the `t2smc` hwmon.
- `modprobe -r sbs` also unloads `sbshc`; load it again before `insmod t2sbs.ko`.
- The charge limit works (`charge_control_end_threshold`; the SMC's `CHLS`, which
  it keeps across reboots). It stops charging about 5 points below the limit and
  starts again some 10 points below: a limit of 90 holds the battery at 80-85 %.
  macOS may change it, so `t2-charge-limit.service` sets it again at every boot.
- The fan daemon saves its config on every start. Running it before the package
  is installed leaves a `/etc/t2-fancontrol/config.txt` with upstream defaults,
  and pacman then puts the shipped config in `config.txt.pacnew`.
